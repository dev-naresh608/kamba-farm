The Story & Origin expand_more

Turmeric is one of our main crops. We grow several varieties between our tree rows, in the filtered light beneath the trees: it is a tropical understory plant and does not need full sun. We leave it in the soil for two years so the fingers grow larger and mature fully, then process it here at the farm, by hand and with great care, from the fresh rhizome to the finished powder.

Storage & Shelf Life expand_more

Best consumed within 12 months. Store in airtight glass container away from direct sunlight. Do not introduce wet spoons.
