The farm

Tree crops, intercrops and well over 300 plant species on an organic farm near Nasrapur.

LAND & ECOLOGY · NEAR NASRAPUR, PUNE

Many tree crops, and nothing leaves the land

We grow many different tree crops. During the early years, before the tree canopy closes, we use the spaces between the tree rows for crops that need more light, including vegetables and grasses. We do not grow the traditional field crops of our part of India: there is no millet, rice or wheat at Kambafarm.

Today the farm has somewhere between 75 and 100 different species of fruit-bearing, nut-bearing and other harvest-bearing trees, and well over 300 plant species in all. Some provide a harvest; others support the developing ecosystem and are regularly cut and mulched to feed and protect the soil.

We follow one strict rule: not a single piece of organic matter should leave our land. Everything that grows here and is cut back is returned to the soil, where it becomes part of the farm's continuing cycle of growth and regeneration.
