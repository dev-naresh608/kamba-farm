Our animals

Species-appropriate care for every animal on the farm, with mothers and babies raised together.

Who lives here

At night all our animals stay in secure sheds or enclosures, because leopards visit the area regularly. During the day they are free to move and forage.

[ MILK GOATS ]

Goats

A herd of milk goats approaching 20 animals, many still young. Fresh goat milk is planned from next year.

[ POND DUCKS ]

Ducks

Muscovy and regular ducks with a pond of their own. Their eggs are larger, with a big yolk, and great for baking.

[ COUNTRY CHICKENS ]

Chickens

Desi chickens that roam freely during the day. Their eggs are for our household and for sale when available.

[ GOOSE COUPLE ]

Geese

A goose couple that shares the pond and the farmyard with the ducks.

[ SEVEN DOGS & TWO CATS ]

Dogs and cats

Fledermaus, Athos, Barney, Browny, Lazy, Oona and Latte, plus Nero the black cat and Manya the barn cat.

[ PEACOCKS & WILD VISITORS ]

Wildlife

Between 10 and 15 peacocks live on or visit our land. At Kambafarm, all animals are welcome.

The goat shed

Our goats are well fed, have space to move and live a good goat life on our regenerative farm. When you visit, you are very welcome to see the goat shed and meet the herd.

We cannot describe their milk as organic, because part of their supplementary feed is not organically certified. We would rather be transparent about this than make a claim we cannot substantiate.
