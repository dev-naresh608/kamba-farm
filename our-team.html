Our team

The farm is brought to life by a remarkable local team from the village next to the farm.

People & Stewardship

The people on the farm

Up to 15 women carry out the farming work, caring for the crops and the many regular tasks that keep the farm productive and healthy. Up to 10 men take care of infrastructure and the heaviest work, such as levelling and tilling. Everyone comes from the village close to the farm, and Kambafarm is the only significant local employer.

Co-founder · Animal welfare

Antje

Started Kambafarm with Michael in 2018. Antje had a 30-year corporate career, still leads a small boutique HR consultancy, and holds animal welfare very close to her heart.

Community Kitchen & Preserves

Shobha

Curates zero-waste kitchen batches, fermenting wild greens, wild mangoes, and roselle into seasonal pantry items while leading Saturday table meals.

Nursery & Soil Systems

Dnyaneshwar

Cultivates indigenous Western Ghats saplings, balancing thermal compost piles and microbial teas that recondition degraded soils into spongy humus.

schedule Daily: Bio-inoculant brewing & rootstock budding

Animal Care & Orchard Grazing

Sunita

Oversees holistic pasture rotation for native Osmanabadi goats and free-range poultry, integrating livestock dung directly into orchard nutrient loops.

schedule Daily: Rotational paddock shifts & flock wellness

Harvest & Pune Distribution

Rahul

Coordinates prompt pre-dawn harvests, zero-plastic packaging using dried leaves and twine, and direct route deliveries across residential Pune hubs.

DAILY PRACTICES

The working week

The farm operates six days a week. Sunday is our shared day off.

eco

Regenerative Fieldwork

Daily contour swale maintenance, chop-and-drop tree biomass mulching, and animal paddock rotation to continually feed living soil biology.

soup_kitchen

Harvest & Kitchen Days

Careful hand-picking at dawn and wood-fired simmering for Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday Pune doorstep distributions.

groups

Community & Education

Hosting Saturday educational farm walks, hands-on seed conservation exchanges, and regional farmer sapling distributions.
