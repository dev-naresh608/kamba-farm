How we farm

Permaculture design and syntropic agriculture on a Greencert-certified organic farm.

Our practices

Three core practices

We developed the farm using principles from permaculture design and, even more importantly, syntropic agriculture.

Syntropic agroforestry
01 Practice 01

Syntropic agroforestry

Syntropic agriculture works with the natural succession found in forests and speeds it up through careful human intervention, creating maximum biodiversity and helping dense tree cover develop where there is currently no forest.

  • check_circleLayered strata from ground cover to emergent trees
  • check_circleHeavy mulching from on-farm tree pruning
  • check_circleVegetables and grasses between young tree rows

CANOPY MATRIX · STRATUM 01

Soil regeneration
02 Practice 02

Soil regeneration

Much of our soil health comes from regularly cutting and mulching the supporting tree cover, about twice a year, usually around June and December. Not a single piece of organic matter leaves our land.

  • check_circleCompost teas, including jeevamrut
  • check_circleManure from our own goat herd
