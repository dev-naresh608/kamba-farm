5 Seasonal Farm Meals & Highlights

Friday Dusk · Welcoming Hearth Supper

Slow-simmered organic moong khichdi, kadhi with fresh curry leaves & seasonal chutney.

Saturday Morning · Orchard Breakfast

Hot poha or spiced thalipeeth, fresh free-range pasture duck eggs & wild lemongrass ginger tea.

Saturday Midday · Traditional Village Feast

Woodfire jowar bhakri on chulha, pitla, hand-pounded garlic thecha & chilled roselle cooler.

Saturday Evening · Campfire Courtyard Dinner

Wood-roasted farm brinjals or seasonal paneer, slow fireplace dal & local jaggery dessert.

Sunday Brunch · Harvest Farewell Spread

Fresh tree-ripened papaya, elaichi bananas, garden upma or parathas with fresh churned butter.
