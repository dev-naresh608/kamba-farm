03 · Visiting

Visiting & safety

Important preparations for walking through living agroforestry terrain at Kambafarm.

schedule

Arrival & Timing

Saturday tours begin punctually at 9:30 AM with fresh herbal chai. Please allow 1 hr 45 min from Pune to arrive relaxed without rushing.

hiking

Accessibility & Terrain

Our trails follow natural contour swales and terraced slopes. While paths are wide and graded, they involve uneven soil and slight inclines.

pets

Free-Roaming Dogs & Animals

7 gentle vaccinated rescue dogs move freely on the farm. They welcome calm human contact. Outside visiting pets require prior written consent.

nature

Western Ghats Wildlife

You will encounter harmless reptiles, hares, birds and insects. Always watch where you place your feet and stay on established walking swales.

checkroom

Footwear & Clothing

Closed-toe walking shoes or boots with tread are required. Loose cotton trousers, sun hats, and water bottles are strongly recommended.

smoke_free

Zero Synthetic & Smoke-Free

No smoking or single-use plastics anywhere on the farm. Please take all non-biodegradable personal rubbish back home with you.

04 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers regarding shopping, visits, and agroforestry routines.

When do you harvest and deliver to Pune?expand_more

We deliver to Pune on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday. Harvest occurs at pre-dawn on delivery morning to preserve vitality and volatile aromatic compounds. Place your orders by 6:00 PM the previous evening.

Can I combine food staples and saplings into one order?expand_more

Yes! You can combine kitchen products (turmeric, honey, pickles) and native saplings into a single checkout basket. Saplings are secured in separate crates within the delivery vehicle so roots remain undisturbed.

Are Saturday tours suitable for children?expand_more

Yes, children aged 6 and above thoroughly enjoy meeting the ducks, goats, and learning compost microbiology. Children aged 6-14 receive reduced ticket pricing (₹900). Sturdy shoes are required for all young explorers.

How far in advance should we request a weekend farm stay?expand_more

Because we host only one exclusive party or family per weekend to preserve solitude, dates fill 3-6 weeks in advance, especially during winter and monsoon weekends. Please submit your request early.

How is raw goat milk packaged and delivered?expand_more

Our Osmanabadi goats are milked clean at sunrise. The unpasteurized milk is chilled immediately in sterilized food-grade glass flasks and transported in insulated cold boxes. It must be refrigerated upon receipt and consumed within 48 hours.

Do you offer tours in Marathi and Hindi?expand_more

Yes! Shobha, Dnyaneshwar, and the farm team are native Marathi speakers. Tours can be conducted comfortably in Marathi, Hindi, or English based on the preference of the group.

06 · Certification

Certification & standards

How we farm, how we check it, and how you can see it for yourself.

Kambafarm is certified organic by Greencert

Kambafarm is a Greencert-certified organic farm, and we do not use synthetic chemicals. Our goat milk, when available, will not be sold as organic, because part of the goats' supplementary feed is not organically certified.

spa

Zero synthetic inputs

No chemical fertilisers, pesticides, herbicides or growth regulators are used on any field, orchard or nursery bed. Soil is fed with mulch, compost and on-farm ferments.

verified

Greencert certified organic

The whole farm is organically certified by Greencert. Certificate details are available from the farm office on request.

science

Batch testing

Kitchen products such as turmeric are tested batch by batch before packing. Results are shared on each product knowledge sheet.

visibility

Open to inspection

Every parcel of land, water testing record and soil enrichment method can be seen and discussed during the Saturday farm tour.

description

Want to see our records? Ask the farm office for the latest inspection notes and test reports at connect@kambafarm.in, or on WhatsApp.
