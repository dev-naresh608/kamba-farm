Our story

How a German couple, a team from the village and a piece of land near Nasrapur grew into Kambafarm.

[ ANTJE & MICHAEL ]
Since 2018

Started in 2018 by Antje Bauer and Johannes Michael Nether.

Kambafarm was initiated in 2018 by Antje Bauer and Johannes Michael Nether. We are a German couple who came to India to stay. Antje had a 30-year corporate career and continues to be the managing director of a small boutique HR consultancy. Michael looks back at 30 years of leadership in the ceramic industry and now leads the development of the farm and the Aubingo team.

Growing a diverse agroforestry farm takes patience, particularly when many of the fruit and nut trees need 10 to 12 years before they produce a significant harvest. We are still watching the farm mature, and every year brings something new.

“

“We don't just grow food; we partner with ecological succession and living soil.”

Then and now

From the first plantings in 2018 to a farm that keeps maturing every year.

[ 2018 ]

2018 · The first plantings

We started developing the land in 2018. Many fruit and nut trees need 10 to 12 years before they give a significant harvest.

[ TODAY ]

Today · A maturing agroforestry farm

Somewhere between 75 and 100 fruit-, nut- and other harvest-bearing tree species, and well over 300 plant species across the farm.

Two names, one farm

Distinct identities sharing the same living soil and agroforestry principles.

Aubingo

Our team's cooperative: harvests and kitchen products

AUBINGO Farmer Producer Company Ltd. runs our operations and markets what is grown and processed on the land of Kambafarm. It was created for our employees, who are its sole shareholders: they were essential in building Kambafarm, and Aubingo gives them agency for a future in which they benefit from what has been created. We keep upskilling Aubingo's leaders so they can perform and thrive.
